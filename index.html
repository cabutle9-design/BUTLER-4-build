About My List

Michael Jackson is one of my favorite artists because his music feels timeless. Growing up, my dad, my brothers and I would listen to his music together, and it always brought us joy. His songs include strong vocals, memorable beats, creative videos, and powerful performances. Although Michael has made music across several different decades, many of his songs still feel exciting today. This page highlights a few of my favorite Michael Jackson songs, along with some background on why I love them and how they fit into his overall career.

My Top Michael Jackson Songs

Rock With You

"Rock With You" is a smooth, disco-infused track that captures the essence of the late 1970s. Its catchy melody and romantic lyrics make it a timeless classic that continues to resonate with listeners of all ages.

Anytime I hear this song, it instantly transports me back to my childhood, reminding me of the times I spent dancing around the living room with my family. The song's upbeat tempo and infectious groove always put me in a good mood, and it's a perfect example of Michael Jackson's ability to create music that brings people together and makes them want to dance.

Beat It

"Beat It" stands out because it mixes pop and rock in a way that feels bold, especially for that time period. The guitar solo, the message about avoiding violence, and the energy of the song make it unforgettable.

Fun Fact: MJ requested actual rival gang members to participate in the music video. The making of this song and music video were groundbreaking, as it brought these opposing violent groups together in a positive way.

Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough

This song is a high-energy dance track that showcases Michael Jackson's incredible stage presence and choreography. Its infectious beat and memorable lyrics make it a favorite for both performers and audiences.

When the new Michael movie came out in theaters, I was so excited to see the choreography for this song come to life on the big screen. The way his nephew, Jaafar, recreated the dance moves and the energy of the performance was amazing, and it made me appreciate the song even more.

Black or White

"Black or White" is a powerful anthem that addresses issues of racial equality and unity. Its innovative music video and compelling message have made it a significant part of Michael Jackson's legacy.

This is one of my favorite music videos of all time! As a child, I would watch this video on repeat and try to mimic the dance moves. To this day, I still wish I could have been part of the production cast that sang and danced alongside Michael Jackson and Macaulay Culkin in the video.

Albums

Off the Wall

Off the Wall helped Michael Jackson become a major solo artist. The album combined disco, pop, funk, and soul, which helped it appeal to a wide audience. Popular songs from this album include "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough" and "Rock with You".

  • Released in 1979
  • Known for its innovative production and danceable beats
  • "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough" won Michael Jackson his first Grammy Award
  • Michael asked Quincy Jones to produce the album after they met working on the film "The Wiz"

Thriller

Thriller is Michael Jackson's most successful album with an estimated 70 million copies sold worldwide, featuring iconic tracks like "Billie Jean" and "Beat It". It became the best-selling album of all time and revolutionized music videos.

  • Released in 1982
  • Known for its innovative production and danceable beats
  • The album was almost titled "Midnight Man"
  • On the original full vinyl cover, a tiger cub is sitting on Michael's leg.

Bad

Bad continued Michael Jackson's success after Thriller. The album had a stronger pop and rock sound, and several songs became major hits, including "Smooth Criminal" and "The Way You Make Me Feel". It solidified Michael's status as a global superstar.

  • Released in 1987
  • The song "Bad" was originally intended to be a duet with Prince in response to fan-base rivalry.
  • Following the intense tabloid scrutiny, the video for "Leave Me Alone" was created to mock the rumors about him.
  • The Bad World Tour was his first solo tour, playing to 4.4 million people and becoming the highest-grossing tour if its time.

Dangerous

Dangerous marked a shift in Michael Jackson's musical style, incorporating new jack swing and R&B elements. The album featured hits like "Black or White" and "Remember the Time". This album also showed how he used music to discuss social issues, unity, and change.

  • Released in 1991
  • The album was recorded over 17 months, from June 1990 to October 1991, across seven different studios, with Jackson often working 18-hour days.
  • The music video for "Black or White" was one of the most expensive ever made at the time, costing around $4 million.
  • The Dangerous World Tour was his second solo tour, playing to 3.5 million people worldwide.

History: Past, Present and Future, Book I

History: Past, Present and Future, Book I is a compilation album that includes some of Michael Jackson's greatest hits along with new material. It was released in 1995 and features songs like "Scream" and "You Are Not Alone". The album serves as a reflection on his career and legacy.

  • Released in 1995
  • The album includes two discs: one with greatest hits and another with new material.
  • "You Are Not Alone" was the first song to debut at number one on the Billboard Hot 100 chart.
  • The song "Scream" was recorded with his sister Janet Jackson. The music video holds the record for the most expensive music video ever made at the time.

Blood on the Dance Floor

Blood on the Dance Floor is a remix album that includes five new songs and eight remixes of previous hits. It was released featuring tracks like "Blood on the Dance Floor" and "Morphine". The album showcases Michael's continued creativity and willingness to experiment with his sound.

  • Released in 1997
  • The album includes five new songs and eight remixes of previous hits.
  • Songs on the album, such as "Morphine" and "Ghosts" address pain, medication, and emotional struggles while dealing with his public image.
  • The album was created partly to fulfill a contract with Sony and to extend the History era, as the label wanted more material while Michael preferred only releasing new songs as an EP.

Invincible

Invincible is Michael Jackson's final studio album. It features a mix of pop, R&B, and hip-hop influences, with songs like "You Rock My World" and "Butterflies". The album reflects Michael's continued evolution as an artist and his desire to stay relevant in the changing music landscape.

  • Released in 2001
  • The album was recorded over a period of three years, from 1997 to 2000.
  • It featured work from ten different record producers and over 100 musicians, including Rodney Jerkins, Teddy Riley, and Babyface.
  • Because production lasted over four years, Jackson recorded an overwhelming amount of music. Out of more than 50 tracks recorded, only 16 made the final cut.

Michael Jackson's Musical Impact

Michael Jackson's impact on music and popular culture is difficult to overstate. Throughout his career, he helped break racial barriers in the entertainment industry and changed the way people viewed music videos by turning them into cinematic storytelling experiences. He also raised expectations for live performances through his unique dance moves, stage presence, and attention to detail. His creativity and willingness to push boundaries influenced artists across many different genres, and his contributions can still be seen in today's music industry. Even years after his peak success, Michael Jackson remains one of the most influential entertainers of all time.

  • Michael Jackson is often referred to as the "King of Pop" due to his immense influence on the genre and his record-breaking success.
  • He was one of the first black artists to gain widespread popularity on MTV, helping to break down racial barriers in the music industry.
  • His music videos, such as "Thriller" and "Bad", set new standards for production quality and storytelling in the medium.
  • Michael's signature dance moves, like the moonwalk, have become iconic and are still emulated by performers today.
